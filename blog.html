A NOTE TO MY FUTURE SELF

I want this space to change with me.

I do not want this journal to become a collection of perfectly polished opinions that never evolve. I want to be able to come back six months later, read something I wrote, and understand what changed.

Some answers will become clearer. Some will become more complicated. A few will probably be completely different. That, to me, is a sign of growth.

Marie

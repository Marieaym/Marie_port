A PERSONAL ARCHIVE · MY TAKE

Questions.
My answers.

This is where the portfolio slows down. I start with a question, then answer it from where I am now — honestly enough to show a point of view, and openly enough to let that point of view evolve.

Not a FAQ.

A collection of beliefs, lessons, opinions and unfinished thoughts — organized around the things I care about.
01
AI & TECH

What should AI look like in Africa?

Context should come first.

I do not think there is one “African AI.” Africa is too diverse for that. AI built for African contexts should begin from local realities instead of treating them as an afterthought. Cost, connectivity, language, trust and everyday habits influence whether a product is useful.

02
AI & TECH

Why do local languages matter so much?

Language is access.

Working on Hausa and Zarma made datasets feel less abstract to me. Language determines who can participate fully in digital systems. Inclusion is also a technical challenge involving data, evaluation, tooling and community participation.

03
AFRICA & IMPACT

Can technology really create social impact?

Only when the problem comes first.

Building an app does not automatically create impact. Start with people, understand the problem, test with reality, measure something meaningful and be willing to change the product when reality tells you that your idea is wrong.

04
LEADERSHIP

What does good leadership mean to me?

Responsibility before title.

Being President of my Youth Leadership cohort and a Co-lead in TCC are teaching me that leadership is mostly responsibility: communicating clearly, listening, following through and creating room for other people to contribute.

05
LEADERSHIP

What would make me a great boss someday?

Make people better, not smaller.

I would want to lead in a way that gives people clarity, trust, feedback and room to grow. I want to be demanding about the quality of the work without making people feel that their worth depends on one imperfect result.

06
CHARACTER

How do I become a good person while still being ambitious?

Success should not cost me my values.

Ambition matters to me. So do kindness, integrity, humility and generosity. I want to be proud of what I achieve and also of how I behaved while achieving it.

07
PERSONAL BRAND

What do I want people to remember when my résumé is closed?

The way I think. The way I treat people.

Personal branding should not be a performance. I want people to associate my name with curiosity, thoughtful work, reliability, warmth, technical growth and a willingness to build.

08
CONFIDENCE

How do I become confident without becoming performative?

Visibility without losing softness.

I am learning that confidence does not require becoming the loudest person in a room. It can look like preparation, calmness, speaking when you have something to contribute and being comfortable letting your work speak too.

09
STUDENT LIFE

How do I think about ambition?

Big goals, sustainable pace.

There are always more courses, applications, competitions and projects. I am learning to choose rather than chase everything. I would rather build meaningful things and understand why I am doing them than collect opportunities because they look impressive.

10
GROWTH

What have setbacks taught me?

Progress is allowed to be nonlinear.

The IOAI eligibility issue, difficult technical problems and projects that needed several attempts taught me to separate a disappointing outcome from a judgement about my ability. I can be disappointed and still continue.

11
FUTURE

What do I want to contribute to Niger’s tech ecosystem?

Stay rooted. Think globally.

I want to contribute as a builder who understands the local context. I would love to see more products, research and companies created from here, with the ambition to solve local problems and still reach wider markets.

12
CHARACTER

What does success mean to me?

A life I can respect.

I want success to include useful work, independence, growth, strong relationships, contribution and the freedom to keep becoming. I do not want an impressive résumé to be the only evidence that my life is going well.

STILL BECOMING

I don’t want
perfect answers.

I want thoughtful ones — and the freedom to come back later and see how I changed.

WHO I AM · BEYOND THE CV

The person behind
the portfolio.

I am an Artificial Intelligence student from Niamey learning to turn curiosity into work, ambition into discipline and ideas into things people can actually use. I care about technology, leadership and impact — and equally about the person I become while pursuing them.

“I want a life that is ambitious enough to stretch me, grounded enough to keep me honest, and useful enough to matter.”

THINGS THAT SHAPED ME

People. Places. Moments. Questions.

My story is not only made of achievements. It is shaped by where I come from, the people who encouraged me, the realities I see around me, the projects that worked, the opportunities that changed direction and the questions that stayed.

Being close to real problems has made me want an education that is technically strong but useful. That is why agriculture, healthcare, local languages, civic technology and information integrity keep appearing in my work.

THEN · NOW · NEXT

A life in chapters.

Then. A student discovering AI, experimenting and asking questions.

Now. A builder, student leader, competitor, youth representative and work-in-progress.

Next. Graduate, deepen AI/ML/Data Science, build with purpose and grow into larger rooms.

MY PERSONAL OPERATING SYSTEM

How I try to work.

When I don’t know → I learn.Curiosity is more useful to me than pretending I already have the answer.
When I see a problem → I build.I like turning vague ideas into something concrete enough to test.
When I fail → I analyse.I want setbacks to become information, not identity.
When I lead → I listen.Leadership should leave room for other people to contribute.
When I succeed → I stay curious.A result matters, but the question after the result matters too.
When I am unsure → I still try.Preparation matters; so does the courage to apply before feeling perfectly ready.

WHAT I BELIEVE

Some things I want to build my life around.

Technology should not only be advanced. It should be useful.
Ambition and humility can exist in the same person.
Where you come from should influence what you build, not limit what you can become.
Leadership is not about being the loudest person in the room.
Representation matters in technology.

MY DEFINITION OF SUCCESS

To build a life
I can respect.

Useful work, good relationships, independence, continued learning, contribution and the freedom to keep becoming.

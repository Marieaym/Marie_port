# How I update The Journal

1. Open `journal-data.js`.
2. Copy one existing article object.
3. Give it a unique `id`.
4. Write the English and French title, excerpt and article body.
5. Add the category and date.
6. Save the file and open `blog.html` to review it.
7. Push the change to GitHub. Vercel redeploys the site automatically when the repository is connected.

The Journal is intentionally separated from the portfolio: the portfolio documents what I have done; the Journal documents how I think.
